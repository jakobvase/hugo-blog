# Ideas for games

## Incremental but there's no game

Incremental but there's no game, it's just a speedrun through an upgrade tree.
Option to add several things like "limit clicks/upgrade to 5", "all upgrades
single click", "enable dependent upgrade (need these two for that one)". Also
option to add upgrades to the tree that actually do things, like little cursors
that move around and click things for you. Also option to enable users to
configure the size and depth of the tree. Also option to make the individual
silly buttons do things (gain 1 orange, then an orange falls to the bottom of
the screen, or to the bottom of a cylinder, or something of the sort. And a
button that says fix glitches, where the whole screen glitches out when you
press it). Needs to feel good to click things, need to have a precise timer at
the top. Need to let users use random seeds.

## Get-out-of-depression incremental

Incremental games always revolve around getting bigger/more powerful/more
something. Would be interesting to make a game where you start out as really
depressed and the goal is to get out of bed and go outside. Could have a sober
vibe. Each thing you can do is like "try to move fingers. Cost: 50 energy.
Chance to succeed: 5%. Failure: self-loathing + 100, self-pity + 50. Success:
hope + 10, self-loathing + 20". "I can't even move my fingers?", "I move my
fingers, and that's a success?". Then the meta upgrade tree is things like
"celebrate your victories (-10% self-loathing on victories)" etc.

## Time loop tribe space rocket

You're a person in a tribe. Go through life normally (go hunting, find wood,
mate, etc) then die. Next life is the same, but everything you did before is now
5 times faster. And compounding, so you do everything you did before, but you're
only 20. Then you choose what now, with all the things unlocked. End goal is to
do enough stuff to escape the time loop/send off a rocket/etc etc.

Also possibility to add teaching, where you teach the other tribe members things
and the next iteration you can teach them faster. So they can better get food
for you, help you with science, gather materials for building the rocket, etc.

The nice thing here is that there's no meta tree. Upgrades are so fast that next
time they're gone in the blink of an eye, so there's even no need to let you
change what you did before.
